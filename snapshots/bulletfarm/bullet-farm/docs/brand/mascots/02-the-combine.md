# 2. The Combine

**Why this wins.** This is the “many minds, one verified line to main” mascot, and it is the strongest *story* sticker for a README hero. Bullet Farm looks like a harvest: messy wild stalks (agent attempts) go in; one twine-bound sheaf of verified commits comes out. A cute, compact combine — half farm machine, half friendly robot — is instantly merchable, slightly funny, and distinctive in the agent-tool logo pile of rockets and robots. Developers will read it in one second: you do not ship the weeds.

## Image prompt (paste this)

A die-cut sticker illustration of a compact, friendly combine harvester character, low and wide like a toy tractor, with a visor of dark glass, two round lamp-eyes, and a small chimney that puffs a single clean check-shaped puff of steam — the check is a shape, not a letter. The header reel on the left is pulling in wild, messy stalks: frayed PR ribbons, broken branch forks, and faint ghostly terminal rectangles, all muted dust-brown and visually subordinate. Out of the rear chute on the right comes one tight golden wheat sheaf bound with a signal-green ribbon that looks like a rail, not a flag. The machine body is cream enamel and brass rivets, chunky wheels with chevron treads, thick rounded black outline, cel shading, four or five flat colors, no photoreal metal, no brand badges, no readable words. Centered, slight 3/4 view from the front-left so the “in weeds / out sheaf” read is left-to-right. Transparent background, sticker margin, designed to look expensive on a laptop lid and still parse at small README size.

## Negative prompt

photoreal John Deere copy, real trademark logos, blood, fire, explosions, Mad Max rig, crowded town, cowboy, skull, tiny text, isometric farm map, extra vehicles, humans, guns, watercolor, neon city

## Sticker / logo notes

- Left-to-right story is mandatory; do not center the machine so both ends crop off.
- Die-cut follows the machine plus the sheaf; drop the incoming weeds if they make a weak outline.
- Palette: `#F4E7C8` cream, `#C4A35A` brass, `#6B5344` dust, `#E6B422` harvest gold, `#3DDC84` rail green.
