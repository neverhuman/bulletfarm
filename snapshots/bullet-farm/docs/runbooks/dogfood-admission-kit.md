# Dogfood admission kit (operator)

Status: **PROPOSED — NOT EXECUTABLE.** Engineering consumers and independent review are incomplete.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-07

Owner: the operator, after reviewable engineering inputs exist

Authority: [ADR 0015](../decisions/0015-dogfood-track.md) and OPEN OD-K in
[ADR 0013](../decisions/0013-operator-decision-register.md).

The selected route is a preserved fresh internal-development coordinator generation, followed by
Codex, Claude, and Cursor subscription coding dogfood. This kit does not lift the Operating HOLD,
ratify OD-K, recover the original incident, or authorize a provider invocation. Agents may implement
and test bounded consumers using manual path-exact coordination; they never create operational keys,
enrollments, or operator-decision records. Test fixtures are not operator artifacts.

## Actual engineering state

The Hub contains typed dogfood contracts, purpose-separated signer validation, policy/binding and
release refusals, incident and W0 producers, sealed-file publication, and a diagnostic readiness board.
Kernel exposes a Claude-only proposal composition, runtime inspection, and create-once artifact
producers. These are components. Their existence does not establish durable launch admission,
complete runtime isolation, native conformance, a Candidate, or a successful coding transaction.

The board keeps `--track coord|dogfood|all`. Its `configuration` view separately reports binding,
policy, and the three required providers. Its `operation` view requires observed operation; release
status remains independent. Missing policy cannot be replaced by a binding. Enrollment, runtime,
conformance, containment, durable-launch and operational read-back checks currently report
`UNVERIFIED` with named `DOGFOOD_*_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE` blockers. There is no provider-ready result yet.

The September 7 review found these fresh-start gaps:

- CLI initialization can omit its W0/inventory inputs; when present, their published references are
  discarded rather than bound into durable Genesis and validated on restart.
- The consumer does not verify every retired inventory, exact replay, review-record bytes and tree
  subject under the final initialization guard.
- W0's former parser ignored heartbeat and receipt semantics. Strict replay must reject incomplete
  framing and work without a disposition. Expired claims alone do not prove safe retirement.
- One inventory describes one incident root, while the former ceremony relocates two. Both complete
  locations, including all quarantine bytes, must be part of one reviewed admission packet.

A source correction may close a listed component defect only with observed proof. No list in this
kit can substitute for the typed inventory or authorize a transition.

## Checkpoint A: new coordinator, preserved incident

Complete the consumers and refusal tests in ADR 0015's September 7 amendment before presenting the
operator with a concrete approval packet. That packet must bind both original incident locations and
expected retirement destinations, complete file inventories, strict replay, every unresolved-work
disposition, the four current commit/tree pairs and repository identities, and independent review
record bytes. Keep the original incident unresolved; explicitly quarantined historical work must not
be adopted as a new lease or described as integrated.

The guarded initialization must refuse omitted/changed inputs, partial retirement, destination
collision and concurrent subject drift. It must retain admission references in Genesis, return the
same identity on exact retry, reject changed retry, and revalidate references after restart. Proof
must cover conflict rejection, owner-bound heartbeat, expiry, stale generation, handoff, exact
integration receipt and reconstruction. Current producers alone do not satisfy this checkpoint.

The operator then reviews the exact amendment and packet and independently performs the checkpoint.
A different reviewer must verify actual bytes; choosing a second principal label is not independent
review. Only the admitted procedure can relocate incident paths or initialize a distinct generation.
Never invent `CURRENT`, chmod frozen incident bytes, or call a fresh generation recovery.

After restart read-back, use the new coordinator for one bounded reviewed docs/test change. Human
approval controls its integration. Record the actual integration subject and reconstruct the claim,
proof, handoff and receipt after restart. Only then is coordination dogfood demonstrated.

## Checkpoint B: provider enrollment and durable launch

This checkpoint follows complete generated-contract consumers, supervised SQLite upgrades, durable
command admission and fake-provider full-composition tests. One transaction must validate revision
and idempotency, reserve finite capacity, consume the launch nonce, allocate the run and create its
Runner outbox item. Restart or response loss must return the same invocation, never launch another.

Enroll each chosen subscription account separately. Bind the exact provider, protocol, model/effort,
account commitment, version, runtime dependency closure, policy, snapshot, gates, budget and deadline.
Use Codex App Server with version-derived schemas/model discovery, Claude headless stream JSON, and
Cursor ACP. Exact model availability is established by enrollment/calibration. Antigravity follows.

Use separate dogfood launch and enrollment signing purposes. Migrate Kernel's older `provider-runner`
consumer before producing artifacts for the new route; current `produce-policy` output alone cannot
establish compatibility. Keep `live_admission_enabled=false`; the dogfood validator refuses true.
Credentials enter a private provider-specific HOME through an explicit broker projection with controlled
refresh/rotation writes. Never inherit the operator HOME, another provider account, or repository auth
configuration. Complete-runtime passports are mandatory on the admitted route, regardless of path.

Default limits, with tighter operator/provider/resource limits winning:

| Resource | Initial bound |
| --- | --- |
| Active invocations | One per enrolled account |
| Implementation workers | At most two on this host, subject to admission |
| Cold builds | One, with measured build capacity plus 8 GiB scratch margin |
| Task repair/escalation | At most two repairs and one expert escalation |
| Whole bounded task | Eight total provider invocations and 60 minutes, including planning/review |

Record subscription billing mode, observed usage, estimated equivalent cost and actual charges as
different facts. Missing monetary cost is `UNPRICED`; unknown quota is not unlimited. Never silently
switch to API billing or another account. Preserve unknown usage after ambiguous failure; release
process/concurrency reservations only after termination is established. Test timeout, acknowledged
cancellation, runtime/account drift, credential rotation, descendants and teardown before native runs.

## Checkpoint C: coding campaign and human integration

Start with fixed planner → implementer → reviewer orchestration. Use deterministic tools first,
filtered/calibrated economy or standard models for eligible implementation, and strong systems for
planning, diagnosis and independent review. Budget all roles and repair calls. Different CLI brands
alone do not establish independent underlying model families. Evolutionary authority stays disabled.

Providers return read-only preimage-bound patch proposals. BulletGit alone applies them in private
clones. Sealed catalog gates and separate review qualify one exact Candidate; changed/rebased/repaired
subjects need new proof. Deliver an exact local Candidate ref with expected-old-OID and authoritative
read-back. A human approves integration; record and reverify any changed integration subject.
Proposal-only outcomes remain `PROPOSED`/`REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

The initial campaign is twelve bounded real tasks, four per required CLI as implementer, covering
Rust, TypeScript/React, tests and docs, including one mixed-provider workflow. Exercise restart,
cancellation, duplicates, stale base, provider failure and bounded escalation. Record accepted work,
regressions/reverts, repairs, human minutes, elapsed time, usage and economy implementation-task share
separately from calls/tokens. This campaign is not complete today.

`DOGFOOD_RUN` is an operational observation with no release, transaction or independent-evidence
eligibility. All release profiles retain their own admitted-evidence requirements. No Jeryu action or
forge credential is authorized by this kit; consume Jeryu only through its separately reviewed pin.

## Revocation, custody and rollback prerequisites

Preserve the exact admitted policy, enrollment, review and run bytes. Never edit or delete them in
place, recursively remove the policy directory, or describe unsetting an environment variable as
revocation: default paths may still be loaded. Operator artifacts must use owner-private ancestry,
retained descriptors or an equivalent verified immutable boundary, create-once publication, durable
flush and independent exact read-back. Same-UID labels still do not prove operator identity.

Revocation publishes a successor policy at a new path with strictly greater generation and explicit
key/account revocation. Retain the predecessor, atomically select the successor, restart, and verify
that new launches refuse and existing work follows the admitted cancellation/settlement rule. Record
the exact old/new subjects and observed refusal through the operator-owned checkpoint. Never revive
old authority by restoring a package or database. Supervised SQLite upgrades require verified backup,
known-prefix admission and transactional suffix application; rollback restores verified backup/package
generations while preserving authority high-water, and never runs reverse migrations. Frozen incident
bytes remain untouched.
