# 4. The One-Rail Tractor

**Why this wins.** This is the “we actually land on main” mascot. Rail is a farm tractor that has decided it is also a locomotive: one sealed crate, one track, no side quests. Behind it, dusty alternative paths fade; the single brass rail ahead is clean. The image explains Bullet Farm’s own protected-integration story without making a claim about another product. Perfect for release notes, `just demo`, and a hex sticker that says *delivery*, not merely activity.

## Image prompt (paste this)

A die-cut sticker of a squat farm tractor fused with a tiny locomotive, facing right, as if it were a single toy. Big rear chevron-tread wheel, smaller front wheel riding a single raised brass rail that runs straight through the frame; no second rail, no switches. On the flatbed behind the cab sits one sealed wooden crate bound with signal-green steel strapping and a wax-like brass seal (a blob with a fence-notch, no letters). The cab has a round visor and two lamp-eyes; a short stack puffs one neat cloud. The tractor enamel is cream, the mechanical bits brass, the rail signal-green on top and brass on the side. In the far lower-left background only, two or three faded dirt tracks peel away into abstract dim porch bulbs and stacked ghost terminals — small, desaturated, clearly unselected alternatives. Thick sticker outline, cel shade, five colors max, no photoreal rust porn, no weapons, no readable depot signs. Centered with space around the rail ends so the die-cut can include a short track.

## Negative prompt

Thomas the Tank Engine, real John Deere, real railroad logos, Mad Max war rig, guns, spikes, skulls, crowded railyard, cowboy, mayor, readable signage, photoreal, 3D cinema, extra cargo, humans

## Sticker / logo notes

- Motion is always to the right (toward `main`).
- Die-cut: wheels + crate + a short rail tongue. Drop the ghost streets from the cut path.
- Palette: `#F4E7C8` cream, `#C4A35A` brass, `#3DDC84` rail, `#1B2A22` ink, `#6B5344` dust.
