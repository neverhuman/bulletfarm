# 5. The Barn-Owl Attestor

**Why this wins.** Independent verification requires a principal that neither writes the Candidate nor trusts writer-produced proof. Attestor is a barn owl that does not write code and does not drive the tractor — it only stamps. A pale facial disk like a witness lamp, a brass signet on one talon, and a blindfold that is *half* lifted: it is blind to the author’s workspace and open only to the reconstructed Candidate. It is the security-community sticker (owls already read as “watchful”), and it looks expensive in one-color ink without needing a product comparison.

## Image prompt (paste this)

A die-cut sticker mascot of a compact barn owl perched on a short fence post, facing the viewer, facial disk a pale cream lamp, ink-dark eyes, small brass signet ring on the right talon ready to stamp a blank wax wafer that has a hexagonal nick and no letters. A narrow signal-green blindfold sits across the brow and is lifted only over the left eye, reading as “blind to the writer, open to the evidence,” not as injury or pirate costume. Chest feathers are cream chevrons; wings folded, one primary feather tipped brass like a nib. The fence post is a single hexagonal brass pillar, not a farm clutter set. Behind and below, very small, abstract overlapping branch shadows and an unbound status light are stamped out by a faint green ring — keep it 10 percent of the frame. Flat cel-shaded, thick rounded outline, four colors, no horror owl, no blood, no sheriff badge, no books with titles. Transparent background, strong midnight contrast for GitHub dark mode, silhouette that still works in one-color black.

## Negative prompt

photoreal owl, horror, blood, skull, pirate, wizard, graduation cap, police badge, cowboy, saloon, mayor, weapons, tiny text, extra birds, Hedwig copy, neon city, watercolor blur

## Sticker / logo notes

- One-color merch test: if the facial disk + signet still read in black vinyl, the mark is good.
- Do not give the owl a keyboard or a welding torch — that collapses attestor into implementer.
- Palette: `#F4E7C8` cream, `#1B2A22` ink, `#C4A35A` brass, `#3DDC84` signal green.
