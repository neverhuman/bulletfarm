# 1. Fence the Goat

**Why this wins.** GitHub already loves a simple animal with a job (Ferris, the gopher, the Octocat). Fence is a stout farm goat standing *inside* a glowing hexagonal paddock, which is the whole Bullet Farm thesis in one silhouette: isolation is the product. Fence’s joke is physical — outside the hex, abstract unresolved branches, terminal panes, and overlapping attempt footprints fade at the rail; inside, one brass bell (a cartridge standing in for a fence token) hangs quiet on the goat’s collar. The contrast depicts Bullet Farm’s own rejected attempts, not another product. Readable at 32px, die-cut at 3 inches, and immediately “I want that on my laptop.”

## Image prompt (paste this)

A die-cut vinyl sticker mascot of a compact, muscular farm goat standing dead-center inside a slightly irregular hexagonal fence made of warm brass pipe and a single thin ring of electric-green light, three-quarter view, huge readable silhouette. The goat has cream wool with two ink-dark saddle patches, amber rectangular eyes, a short beard, and a tiny brass cartridge-bell on a leather collar engraved with hash marks — no readable letters. One front hoof pins a snapped, lifeless branch that tried to cross the hex; outside the fence, in the far corners only, an abstract dust field of tangled cables, stacked ghostly terminal panes, and overlapping muddy footprints fades to nothing, never the focus. Flat cel-shaded illustration, thick rounded black outline, four colors only (cream, brass, forest ink, signal green), soft inner shadow, no photoreal fur, no extra animals, no hats, no guns. Centered on a transparent background, generous margin for kiss-cut, works as a GitHub profile sticker and a hex logo.
