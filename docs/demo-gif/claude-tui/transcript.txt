prompt
  Explain Bullet Farm in six short lines: the four member repos, the
  fenced Attempt, exact Candidate identity, independent Evidence, and
  why UNKNOWN is a first-class outcome rather than a green lie.

reply
  The four member repos are independently owned: bullet-farm (hub),
  bullet-kernel (control plane), bullet-git (Git + proof roots), and
  bullet-portal (operator UI) — no single repo can rubber-stamp its
  own work.
  An Attempt is fenced: it runs sandboxed against a pinned corpus,
  with no side channel back into the grading path.
  The Candidate has an exact identity — a specific pinned
  commit/digest, never a branch name — so a result cannot silently
  drift onto the wrong artifact.
  Evidence is gathered independently of the Candidate's own claims;
  the kernel re-derives pass/fail from raw digests rather than
  trusting self-reported status, and gates are fail-closed.
  UNKNOWN is a first-class outcome, not an error: forcing PASS/FAIL
  when the evidence does not support one is the green lie the design
  exists to prevent.
  So UNKNOWN is honest signal — insufficient proof — which is more
  trustworthy than a fabricated green checkmark.
